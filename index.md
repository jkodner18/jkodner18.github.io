<h2>About Me</h2>
<p>⭐ Language Pedagogy • Linguistics • Language Documentation</p>


Hello! I am currently a language teacher at an international school based in Taiwan. I received my MA in linguistics from Harvard University, with much of my research touching upon syntax and its interfaces, primarily syntax-prosody in Altaic languages. During my three-years' time at Harvard, in addition to conducting theoretical linguistics research (see [Linguistics](https://www.kodner.org/linguistics/)), I took classes with the Harvard Graduate School of Education (HGSE), served as a Teaching Fellow for various linguistics and general education courses, and initiated a series of projects dedicated to bringing linguistics to K-12 spaces (see [Teaching](https://www.kodner.org/teaching/)). Before Harvard, I received my BA in Language Science from the University of California, Irvine (UCI) in 2022, and outside of my time in academia I have engaged in a variety of projects dedicated to endangered language documentation in the nonprofit sector.
